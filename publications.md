    <h2>Rethinking Compute Substrates for 3D-Stacked Near-Memory LLM Decoding: Microarchitecture–Scheduling Co-Design</h2>
    <p>Chenyang Ai, Yixing Zhang, Haoran Wu, Yudong Pan, Lechuan Zhao, Wenhui OU</p>

    <p>Chenyang Ai, et al.</p>
