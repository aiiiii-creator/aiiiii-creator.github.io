  <article class="project-card">
    <p class="project-tag">Jan 2023 - Jul 2024</p>
    <h2>Combining the Systolic Array and Vector Unit</h2>
    <p><strong>Role:</strong> Project leader</p>
    <p>Investigated hybrid architectures that combine the strengths of vector-style programmability with systolic-array efficiency.</p>
    <p>This line of work led to two DAC 2025 paper submissions.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <p class="project-tag">Jan 2024 - Jun 2024</p>
    <h2>Heterogeneous SoC Compiler Based on MLIR</h2>
    <p><strong>Role:</strong> Main participant, ranked second</p>
    <p>Converted diverse tensor operators into matrix multiplication through tensor contraction and built an MLIR-based compilation chain.</p>
    <p>The compiler can schedule kernels to different accelerators to improve multithreaded performance and utilization.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <p class="project-tag">Mar 2024 - Present</p>
    <h2>Tensor Train Full-Process Accelerator for LLMs</h2>
    <p><strong>Role:</strong> Project leader</p>
    <p>Explores Tensor Train decomposition for large language models and reuses previous systolic-array experience to design a dedicated accelerator.</p>
    <p>The target architecture accelerates decomposition while supporting vector-matrix multiplication during LLM decoding.</p>
  </article>

  <article class="project-card">
    <p class="project-tag">Apr 2024 - Present</p>
    <h2>Analysis Framework for the Generality of ML Accelerators</h2>
    <p><strong>Role:</strong> Project leader</p>
    <p>Studies how spatial architectures such as systolic arrays and Eyeriss-like designs behave across broader operator spaces.</p>
    <p>The goal is a tensor-algebra-based framework that identifies effective hardware configurations for arbitrary tensor operator ranges.</p>
  </article>
